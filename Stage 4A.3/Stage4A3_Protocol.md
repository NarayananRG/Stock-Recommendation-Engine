# Stage 4A.3A Protocol Freeze

The confirmatory policy is **R3_K1**: the product of frozen TRANSFER `ENTRY_FILLED` and conditional `T1_BEFORE_STOP_63` `LOGIT_FULL` scores, ranked descending by score and then ascending by Signal ID, selecting one same-date candidate. Its sole confirmatory comparator is **R0_K1**, ranked by Actionability Score descending, Technical Score descending, and Signal ID ascending.

Secondary policies R1, R2, R4, R5 and every K2 variant cannot substitute for R3_K1. R5 is an execution-efficiency diagnostic. D0 is secondary. There is no probability threshold, calibration, refit, adaptive feature, ML sizing, ML stop, ML target, or ML exit.

Predictions are written once after 15:45 Asia/Kolkata only when the latest stock and NIFTY bars equal the intended Signal Date. Past dates exist only in dry-run storage. Missed sessions are logged, never backfilled. Zero-candidate sessions still receive snapshots. Outcomes are separate append-only events and never modify prediction files.

Activation is conservative: a Signal Date must be strictly later than the Asia/Kolkata activation calendar date. Every production run verifies the activation commit against the immutable protocol tag, all protected source/config/script/document files, the protocol identity, frozen universe, model manifest, bundle identity, and all seven joblib hashes before it reads candidate data. Both dirty and committed drift are blocked and recorded. Activation and tagging are intentionally outside this hardening change.

Production input is not operator-supplied. The after-close runner refreshes the frozen 19-ticker universe through the exact Stage 2.1 signal engine, Stage 2.2.1 execution wrapper, Stage 2.2.2 Signal-ID function, and Stage 3.1 signal-time feature/opportunity functions. It admits only `BASELINE_PRIMARY`, extracts the frozen FS2/FS3 contracts, and preserves raw-data, NIFTY, builder, universe, Signal-ID-set, feature-contract, and full-input hashes in `candidate_input_manifest.json`. Arbitrary `--input` and `--market-manifest` arguments are dry-run only.

Outcome resolution accepts no typed label values. The resolver derives ENTRY, T1, and T2 terminal events with the frozen Stage 3.1 entry-window, label-availability, STOP_FIRST, and 63-session rules. It derives D1 and D0 policy results through the frozen Stage 2B.1 portfolio engines, past-only annual calibration, costs, 0.75% risk, 25% position cap, five-position cap, no leverage, and 63-session maximum hold. Events are stored as immutable timestamped batches under each Signal Date and linked by one global event hash chain. A terminal `(Signal ID, Policy, Outcome Type)` cannot be rewritten.

Final evaluation remains locked until all gates pass: 24 calendar months, 150 BASELINE_PRIMARY candidates, 50 completed D1 trades for each of R0_K1 and R3_K1, 100 resolved entry labels, 60 filled opportunities with resolved T1 outcomes, a valid ledger chain, and no protocol drift. There is no force option.

Every count is derived from the snapshot and outcome ledgers; production accepts no counts file. Before unlock, the status report exposes operational counts only and prohibits performance fields. After unlock, the evaluator creates the complete preregistered predictive, policy, random-control, paired-bootstrap, sensitivity, criteria, and report artifact set. The test-only `SYNTHETIC_MATURED_PROSPECTIVE_FIXTURE` proves the locked and unlocked paths without creating a real prospective observation.

Final economic results never compound terminal trade-event fractions. After the gate passes, one frozen Stage 2.2.1 adapter archives complete daily OHLC history for the frozen universe and NIFTY, with file and logical hashes. One common frozen Stage 2B.1/Stage 2.2.2 execution path then produces trades, orders and daily equity for D1 `TRAIL_ONLY`, D0 `STATIC_COMPAT`, named policies and exactly 500 K1 random controls. The portfolio is flat with ₹100,000 on the first valid session strictly after activation, includes every valid cash and zero-candidate session, and ends on the final As-Of Date. Provider revision risk remains a limitation; the immutable archived data is authoritative for the final analysis.

The paired bootstrap aligns R3_K1 and R0_K1 on the identical daily session calendar. It samples contiguous non-circular blocks with valid starts `0..n-block_length`, shared paired indices, 2,000 replicates, seed 42, primary length 63 and sensitivities 21 and 126. Random-control percentiles use the Stage 4A.2 mid-rank tie rule. T2 has counts and prevalence only because the frozen seven-model bundle has no T2 predictor. ENTRY uses R5 and conditional T1 uses the frozen TRANSFER T1 LOGIT_FULL component.

On every production after-close run, the NIFTY session calendar is compared with the immutable snapshot index. Missing valid sessions are recorded once as `MISSED` in a separate tamper-evident coverage chain and are never backfilled. Zero-candidate snapshots are `CAPTURED`. Operational session counts come from this ledger.

R3_K1 confirms positive prospective economic utility only if all ten preregistered criteria pass. These include superiority on total return, CAGR and expectancy R; non-inferior profit factor; a positive 2.5% bound from the paired 63-session moving-block bootstrap; at least the 95th percentile of 500 label-blind K1 controls; drawdown no more than two percentage points worse; at least 50 completed trades; ledger integrity; and no protocol drift. Failure keeps Stage 5 blocked.
