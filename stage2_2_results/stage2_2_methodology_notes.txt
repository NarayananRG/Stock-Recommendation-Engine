Research candidate horizon: T1 with a 63-session maximum.
R:R research bands: <1.5, 1.5-1.99, 2.0-2.49, 2.5-2.99, 3.0+.
Intraday pullback entry-bar target-only touches are not credited.
Gap-down pullback limit orders execute at the better opening price; orders whose executed entry invalidates the stop/target ordering are rejected.
Capacity is freed by opening-gap exits, but not reused after an unknown-time intraday exit on the same session.
Daily open-position valuation uses unadjusted close mark-to-market; slippage and transaction cost are realized only at execution.
