# Promotion Gates

No Stage 6 component gains production authority merely because tests pass. Promotion is explicit, component-specific, reversible, and requires an independently reviewed contract change.

Minimum evidence includes deterministic and reproducible implementation, point-in-time safety, provenance and identity integrity, historical evaluation where appropriate, prospective shadow evidence, comparison against the frozen Stage 5D.5 control, sufficient event and trade counts, turnover/risk/drawdown evaluation, adverse and missing-data failure-mode testing, and operational monitoring with rollback criteria.

Calibration, event classification, exposure mapping, analogue analysis, thesis review, portfolio constraints, and morning revalidation are evaluated separately. Evidence for one component cannot promote another. Discovery-only wins, small samples, unreproducible improvements, future leakage, or degraded failure behavior block promotion. No component may be promoted if it materially worsens safety, capital preservation, auditability, or production reliability.
