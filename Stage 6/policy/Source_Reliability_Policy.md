# Source Reliability Policy

Authority descends as follows: `PRIMARY_OFFICIAL` > `AUTHORITATIVE_INDEPENDENT` > `DISCOVERY` > `UNVERIFIED`.

- `PRIMARY_OFFICIAL`: NSE, BSE, SEBI, RBI, Indian ministries, company exchange filings, company investor relations, WTO or official tariff authorities, and EIA/PPAC where applicable.
- `AUTHORITATIVE_INDEPENDENT`: accountable professional reporting, rating agencies, audited databases, or established research whose origin and methodology are identifiable.
- `DISCOVERY`: GDELT, aggregators, search indexes, and feeds useful for locating stronger evidence.
- `UNVERIFIED`: unattributed, unverifiable, or provenance-deficient claims.

An official filing proves what was officially disclosed and when; it does not establish the objective truth of every statement. Conflicting official and independent evidence must remain represented. Discovery evidence triggers corroboration and cannot independently alter a portfolio action. Unverified evidence cannot independently alter portfolio actions and must be clearly quarantined. Reliability is attached to each evidence record, not permanently inferred from a hostname.
