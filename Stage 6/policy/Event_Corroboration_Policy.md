# Event Corroboration Policy

Corroboration states are `CONFIRMED`, `CORROBORATED`, `SINGLE_SOURCE_OFFICIAL`, `SINGLE_SOURCE_INDEPENDENT`, `DISCOVERY_ONLY`, `UNVERIFIED`, and `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`.

- `CONFIRMED`: direct primary evidence plus consistent identity and timing.
- `CORROBORATED`: at least two materially independent trustworthy sources agree on the core claim.
- `SINGLE_SOURCE_OFFICIAL`: one primary disclosure exists but independent confirmation is absent or unnecessary.
- `SINGLE_SOURCE_INDEPENDENT`: one accountable independent report exists.
- `DISCOVERY_ONLY`: only a discovery lead exists; interpretation remains provisional.
- `UNVERIFIED`: provenance or authenticity is insufficient.
- `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`: credible sources materially disagree; conflicts remain explicit.

`NON_MATERIAL` may record any clearly labelled quality, including discovery-only evidence, but cannot imply action. `MATERIAL` requires primary official evidence or corroborated authoritative independent evidence. `HIGH` requires strong entity binding, reliable timing, and primary or multiple independent support; conflicts must be surfaced. `CRITICAL` requires the strongest available primary confirmation and independent corroboration where feasible, with explicit uncertainty and escalation for human review. These are evidence-quality requirements, not live action thresholds.
