# Portfolio Diversification Policy

Future portfolio intelligence must evaluate sector concentration, subsector concentration, return correlation, common exposure channels, total portfolio risk, risk at stop, cash reserve, committed and available capital, and maximum positions. Cash is an explicit valid allocation and full deployment is never mandatory.

Stage 6.0 sets no arbitrary production percentages or position limits. Stage 6.5 must preregister candidate limits, test them against the Stage 5D.5 control, measure turnover and drawdown, and document failure behavior before proposing any threshold. Exposure values declare whether they are currency amounts, fractions of capital, or fractions of invested capital. Correlation estimates require method, return frequency, lookback, minimum and actual observations, and data cutoff; missing or unstable estimates must not be interpreted as diversification.
