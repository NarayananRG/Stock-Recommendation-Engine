Paper Portfolio V2 deterministic E2E contract
- D0: two LIVE_DECISION_V1 BUY fixtures create pending orders; cash unchanged.
- D1: next official opens fill whole shares with slippage and transaction costs.
- D2: accepted official closes update marks, NAV, and unrealized P&L.
- D3: EXIT SIGNAL creates a pending next-open exit; no same-close exit.
- D4: next official open fills the exit and records realized P&L.
- Duplicate session/run execution returns the committed result and creates no duplicate order, fill, or journal row.
- Restart uses a new SQLite connection and restores pending orders.
- Injected crash after fill journal insertion rolls back the entire transaction; restart fills exactly once.
