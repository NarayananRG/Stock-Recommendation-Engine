## Binding safeguards

1. None of these rows is an `ACTIVE`, `CONFIRMED` or `MATERIAL` Stage 6 event. The register contains source observations and testable hypotheses only.
2. The `first_known_timestamp` of a machine-ingested event must come from the underlying publication, not the economic period, a retrospective summary or the scrape date.
3. Any `SINGLE_SOURCE_OFFICIAL` classification requires one qualifying official evidence source under `stage6_events.corroboration`. Mere duplication of the same article does not provide independent corroboration.
4. Stage 6.2A does not allow asserted causality or nonempty transmission channels. Any cross-sector directional hypothesis belongs in a separately validated shadow-stage mapping.
5. No BUY/SELL decision, position sizing, company scoring or headline-derived fundamental metric can be changed from this register.
6. Later corroboration needs distinct entities, issuer exposure, temporal relevance, publication timestamp, and a historical falsification test.
7. The macro sources describe worldwide effects, not direct evidence that any specific NSE issuer's earnings, valuation or price will change.

## Falsifiable shadow experiments

- Oil supply shock: using PIT oil-price observations and company-level *documented* import/feedstock exposure, test out-of-sample revenue margin deterioration and 20/60-day price dispersion vs exposure-matched controls.
- Financing shock: use PIT RBI yields and official company debt profiles to test whether interest-sensitive firms exhibit incremental risk after controlling for sector/market regime.
- Technology demand shock: test whether issuer-disclosed geographic revenue exposure and actual capex/earnings guidance offer incremental predictive signal over price momentum alone.

Promote a new research rule only after verifying multiple regimes, leakage-free publication dates, sensible null handling, and unchanged hard safety gates. **No measured predictive lift is claimed here.**
